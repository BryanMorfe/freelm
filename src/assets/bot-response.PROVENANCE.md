# FreeLM Bot response chime

Original procedural synthesis created for FreeLM on 2026-10-23. No recordings,
third-party samples, Apple/iMessage audio or external sound assets were used.
Mono PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz, 16 bit, 420 ms. Two soft sine tones at 659.255 Hz and
987.767 Hz, second delayed 70 ms, with 8 ms attack, 85 ms exponential decay,
12% second harmonic and a 25 ms final fade. Peak gains 0.16 and 0.095.

provenance are recorded here; no root project-license choice is made by this asset.
